ABOUT R-VENT

End-to-end mechanical solutions since 2003.

R-Vent HVAC Solutions, Inc. was founded in September 2003 as a PCAB-licensed mechanical contractor. From an agility-focused service group, the company has developed into a full-scale mechanical contracting and asset-management firm serving commercial and industrial HVAC requirements.

Its operational footprint spans commercial and industrial facilities, government complexes, educational institutions, high-density commercial centers and industrial facilities. The mission is to engineer, install and maintain high-efficiency mechanical infrastructure that maximizes equipment life cycles, cuts energy costs and delivers climate precision through technical rigor and uncompromising standards.
